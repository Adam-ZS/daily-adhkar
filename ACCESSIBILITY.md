# Accessibility

Daily Adhkar aims to make Arabic devotional reading usable on small screens, with a keyboard, and with assistive technology. The site uses a right-to-left layout, large touch targets, visible keyboard focus, adjustable reading text, and browser zoom. Counters provide text feedback, and the site respects the device’s Reduce Motion setting.

## Environments and limitations

The hosted site is designed for current mobile and desktop browsers, including Safari on iPhone. Arabic shaping and diacritic appearance depend on the fonts available on the device. The reading text is available with JavaScript disabled. The interactive tabs, counters, and font controls require JavaScript.

Physical iPhone and VoiceOver testing is still pending. The app currently provides Arabic text without audio playback or translations.

## Report a barrier

Open an [accessibility report](https://github.com/Adam-ZS/daily-adhkar/issues/new?template=bug_report.md) and include the page or section, what you were trying to do, and what happened. If you can, include your browser, device, and assistive technology or relevant settings. A screenshot or recording is optional.

Contributors should preserve keyboard access, readable focus states, text resizing, right-to-left layout, and reduced-motion behavior. Update this statement when supported behavior or known limitations change.
